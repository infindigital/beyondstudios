Why it is set up this way

Events never run unattended.

A booth at a wedding cannot be redone. If a printer jams, a camera drifts or a strip comes out wrong, the night is already over and the moment is gone for good.

So every booth booking has an attendant on site. They set it up, keep the line moving, catch the problem before it becomes one and pack it down afterwards.

Studio hire works differently. You are welcome to run it yourself with the full lighting kit included, or ask for a technician and we will confirm availability.

Featured activation

The booth behind sold-out brand launches.

When a brand wants the room talking, the 360 booth goes in. On-brand prints, a custom colour wash and a reel of clips in guests' hands before the night is over.

Activations are the hardest kind of booking to get right. The setup has to read as part of the brand, the line has to keep moving, and there is no second take once the doors open.

It is the reason the booths are built the way they are: rigged, branded and staffed every single time, so a flawless night is the default rather than something we chase.
