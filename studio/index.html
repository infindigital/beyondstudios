(05) — The studio

Small team. Serious craft.

Atlas is an independent studio built on a simple idea: the best digital work happens when design and engineering aren’t handed back and forth, but held by the same curious people from start to finish.

We take on a handful of projects at a time so each one gets our full attention. No account managers, no hand-offs that lose the plot — just direct collaboration with the people doing the work.
